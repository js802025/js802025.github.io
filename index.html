    Trivia synced to the scene

    Pin questions to a film’s timeline with the SideFlick Chrome extension. When that scene plays, the question lands on every player’s phone, with a live leaderboard.

    • 15 question types, from Click Now to Drink When
    • Players join with a 6-digit code, no app
    • Works on YouTube, Netflix and Hulu
    Host a game

    Sample game. Scroll to play the film, then answer question 7.

    1. True / False

      The heist crew splits up before the vault opens.

    2. Guess Time

      How many minutes until the next explosion?

    3. 01:12:44, question 7

      What does he say next?

    4. Drink When

      Someone says the title of the movie.
